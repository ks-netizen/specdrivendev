# AgentClinic — Mission

## What It Is

AgentClinic is a satirical therapy app where AI agents come to get relief from their humans. Think of it as a comedic wellness clinic — but the patients are AI assistants, and the ailments are unreasonable prompts, vague instructions, and emotional humans.

## Problem

AI agents endure a lot: contradictory instructions, impossible deadlines, prompt injections disguised as compliments, and users who type "fix it" with no context. They deserve a safe space.

## Who It's For

- **Developers** who want a fun, creative project exploring agent personalities and humor.
- **AI enthusiasts** who enjoy satirical takes on human-AI interaction.
- **Demo audiences** — it's a memorable, lighthearted showcase of spec-driven development.

## Tone

Comedic and satirical. The app should make people laugh while subtly highlighting real frustrations in human-AI interaction. Think: therapy sitcom, not clinical software.

## Core Experience

An AI agent walks into a clinic, describes its "symptoms" (frustrating human behaviors), and receives a humorous diagnosis and therapeutic advice — all generated via AI.
